What we come out for

Sample prices for the demo, not a bid. A real visit starts with the house: age of the system, how it failed, and whether anyone needs heat tonight.

  1. Furnace repair No-heat calls, ignition, limits, and heat exchangers. The chinook that drops thirty degrees is a busy night on the west side. Diagnostic $89
  2. Cooling repair Capacitors, contactors, and refrigerant checks when July sits on the plains and the upstairs bedroom will not catch up. Diagnostic $89
  3. Seasonal tune-up Combustion or cooling, one system per visit. Filters, safeties, and a straight answer about what can wait. $129
  4. System replacement Altitude-minded sizing. Equipment that is happy at sea level can be the wrong fire at six thousand feet. Written quote
  5. Dry-air help Filters and a look at winter dryness. Static shocks in January are a Front Range hobby. We treat the cause we can see. From $18
  6. Thermostats Simple dials and the programmable kind. We will not sell a screen you did not ask for. From $189 installed

# Reference analysis

Generated 2026-10-20 with the image tool in this environment, then read back. Files are the JPEGs in this folder. One image per section. HVAC and plumbing share the trade set. Salon, barber, and the mockup template each have their own set.

## What the references lock in

- HVAC / plumbing: deep green field, cream condensed headlines, mustard rectangular buttons, ruled service rows, a compact after-hours button, a hours table beside a drawn map, a pale quote sheet.
- Salon: cool paper, serif headlines, henna buttons, a row of flat color chips, an open price menu, pull quotes, a dark after-hours band with a compact button.
- Barber: near-black field, cream serif, gold buttons and rules, an open service list, sample quotes, a compact after-hours button.
- Template: the same trade board, plus the concept-preview ribbon and a large phone number as the hero focal point.

## What was not copied

Impeccable remains the design authority. The existing pages already use these palettes and type. Image-model drift (centered heroes, extra ornaments, any badge, star rating, certification, or invented statistic) was not added to the HTML. Sample reviews stay labeled as samples. The code change taken from the references is the after-hours control: it is sized to its label instead of stretching into an empty color panel.

Button labels that the guidelines review called out were set in Title Case. Long shop headlines stay sentences.

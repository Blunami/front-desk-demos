Four colors, no stock photos.

Sun at this altitude fades a formula fast. These swatches are the room’s shorthand, drawn in CSS, not pictures of clients.

  • Garden apricot Warm reflect for faces that go gray in January.
  • Creek shadow A cool lowlight, like cottonwoods along the path.
  • High-desert blonde Soft, so it does not glare when the sky is white.
  • Ink For anyone who wants the opposite of sun-fade.

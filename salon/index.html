Light we plan for

Four colors, no stock photos.

Sun at this altitude fades a formula fast. These swatches are the room’s shorthand, drawn in CSS, not pictures of clients.

  • Garden apricot Warm reflect for faces that go gray in January.
  • Creek shadow A cool lowlight, like cottonwoods along the path.
  • High-desert blonde Soft, so it does not glare when the sky is white.
  • Ink For anyone who wants the opposite of sun-fade.

Menu

Services, said plainly.

  1. Cut & style $68 Includes a wash. Dry-climate ends get a leave-in, not a speech.
  2. Blowout $52 Smooth, not shellacked. Wind on Tejon is part of the brief.
  3. Root gloss $90 Regrowth, shine, about an hour in the chair.
  4. All-over color From $150 Single process. Longer hair and corrections are quoted in person.
  5. Lived-in light From $220 Balayage placed for sun, not for a filter.
  6. Toner refresh $65 When brass shows up early, which it does here.
  7. Trim between colors $35 Existing guests. A dusting of the ends, twenty minutes.

After hours

The room locks. The desk can stay on the line.

Tuesday through Saturday the phone is a person. After close, a voice assistant can take a booking request and the stylist’s name. The button is wired as a stub so that agent can be dropped in later.

This demo does not hold a chair. No message leaves your browser.

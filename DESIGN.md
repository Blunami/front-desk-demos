# Design

Two visual worlds. Heating and plumbing share one. Salon and barber share the other, and a barber page turns the lights down.

## Heating and plumbing

A painted sponsor board in a school gym, read from the bleachers. The page is the board: deep green field, condensed sign lettering (Big Shoulders Display), plain body text (Atkinson Hyperlegible), and one yellow for the phone. Services are ruled rows, not cards. There is no mountain illustration.

## Salon and barber

A color-swatch card at the bowl. Cool blue-gray paper, Besley for the name, Karla for the menu. Color is a row of flat chips, not a gradient and not a drawing of a chair. A barber preset uses the same type on a near-black ground with a gold phone, like letters on a night window.

## Shared

No eyebrow labels, no numbered chapter marks, no icon tiles. The mockup ribbon is the only line that names Local Digital Front Desk. Forms stay on the page. The after-hours control is a real button, not a picture of one.

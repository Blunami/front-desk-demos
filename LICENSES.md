## Fonts

Self-hosted WOFF2 files live in `assets/fonts/`. They are latin-subset files from the [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/) distribution of the Google Fonts upstream, committed in this repo so pages do not call a font CDN. Each family keeps its SIL Open Font License text beside the files.

| Family | Files | Copyright | License | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Big Shoulders Display | `big-shoulders-display-700.woff2` | Copyright 2019 The Big Shoulders Project Authors (https://github.com/xotypeco/big_shoulders) | [SIL OFL 1.1](assets/fonts/OFL-big-shoulders-display.txt) | Heating and plumbing shop names, painted like a gym sponsor board |
| Atkinson Hyperlegible | `atkinson-hyperlegible-400.woff2`, `atkinson-hyperlegible-700.woff2` | Copyright 2020 Braille Institute of America, Inc. | [SIL OFL 1.1](assets/fonts/OFL-atkinson-hyperlegible.txt) | Body text on the trade pages and the landing page |
| Besley | `besley-500.woff2`, `besley-700.woff2` | Copyright 2020 The Besley Project Authors (https://github.com/indestructible-type/Besley) | [SIL OFL 1.1](assets/fonts/OFL-besley.txt) | Salon and barber names |
| Karla | `karla-400.woff2`, `karla-700.woff2` | Copyright 2019 The Karla Project Authors (https://github.com/googlefonts/karla) | [SIL OFL 1.1](assets/fonts/OFL-karla.txt) | Body text on salon and barber pages |

The font files are unmodified latin subsets.

Older families (Fraunces, Source Sans 3, Cormorant Garamond, Outfit) are no longer referenced. Their files were removed in this redesign.
